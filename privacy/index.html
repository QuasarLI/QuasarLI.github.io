This website

This static website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, accounts, or cookies. It does not collect information through forms.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to provide and secure its service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

External destinations

Links to GitHub, Gumroad, and L’IRC’in leave this website. Those services have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing personal or payment information.

Email

If you email Quasar Labs, the message and your address are used to respond to your inquiry. Do not send secrets, passwords, license keys, or sensitive personal information by email.

Privacy questions can be sent to quasarlabs-inc@outlook.com.
