Plain language

What you may be wondering.

Direct answers about the studio, releases, and this website.

What exactly is Quasar Labs?

Quasar Labs is the parent home for L’IRC’in, TaskForge, Quasar, Cuspr, PixelBridge, and CineDock. Each project keeps its own official destination and release state.

Where do official downloads come from?

Public TaskForge and Quasar downloads come from verified QuasarLI GitHub releases. Paid TaskForge packages are delivered through its named Gumroad products; Quasar’s licensed paid archives are public but require matching Gumroad activation. PixelBridge uses the public PixelBridge-Distribution release, with matching Gumroad keys for paid extras. L’IRC’in and Cuspr use their own official websites. CineDock remains in private development and has no public download.

Does this website track visitors?

No. This static parent site uses no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, accounts, or cookies. Theme choice is stored only for the current browser session.
