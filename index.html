What it does

Guided backup

Step-by-step help connecting your phone, enabling USB debugging and approving the backup on-screen.

Full phone images

Partition-level images with checksums and a manifest. Restore from recovery or with root, with a read-back check after every write.

Compression Lab

Compare Zstandard, Gzip, Bzip2, XZ, ZIP and 7-Zip on size and speed, then use the winner for backups and images.

Encrypted cloud upload

Encrypt on your PC before anything leaves it. Upload to S3-compatible storage, a local folder and more.

Your data stays yours

No account and no tracking. Backups go where you tell them to, and only you hold the passphrase.

Made for Windows

A native desktop app that runs without an installer: unzip it and launch.
