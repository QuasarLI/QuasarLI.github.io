Gaming + streaming · Windows 11 x64

PixelBridge

Stable v1.3.0

A native WinForms launchpad for Xbox Cloud Gaming, GeForce NOW, local emulation, and your own media, requiring .NET Framework 4.8. The non-bundled v1.3 release saves an Edge/Firefox preference in every tier; Firefox Xbox is experimental, requires consent, and offers an explicit Edge fallback. PixelBridge is not an independent cloud rendering service. Sunshine and Moonlight are installed independently; PixelBridge can start/reuse a configured host but never downloads or invokes its installer. No router WAN port forwarding or UPnP is required.

No input or HDMI paywall: saved foreground-only Xbox/XInput menu navigation, all standard controller profiles/native import-export, guided HDMI and user-designated TV hardware checklists are included in every tier. The always-visible menu toggle never disables mouse/keyboard and does not control browsers, Windows or a TV. TV Ethernet alone cannot decode video; extenders/HDBaseT or AV-over-IP need exact compatible hardware. Pro adds advanced digital remap variants, not essential input. Existing matching v1.2 Supporter/Pro purchases remain eligible; both are one-time purchases with indefinitely cached previously verified offline access.

Four production ZIPs and 11 Sigstore signatures were independently verified after release, including the contained EXEs and exact signing workflow SHA. This is not Authenticode or SmartScreen trust. Real service/cloud, customer-key activation, physical controller/TV, gameplay, HDMI/audio and clean-PC pairing acceptance remain untested. No Sunshine, games, ROMs, BIOS files, firmware, or keys are bundled. Original v1.2 downloads and history remain available.

Media center / Windows-first

CineDock

Private development

A media center for your local and mounted network libraries, with readable navigation, original console-inspired visuals, libmpv-based playback, and opt-in Xbox navigation alongside keyboard and mouse. Google Drive, optional saved-HFR conversion, and Sunshine/Moonlight host setup have distinct development and acceptance boundaries.

No public download: CineDock is not a publicly accepted customer release. Its source and owner previews remain private. Browser authorization alone does not establish Drive playback or Google approval; physical-device, Windows, controller, and optional host checks remain open.
