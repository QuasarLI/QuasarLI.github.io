Productivity · Windows

TaskForge

Stable + beta

TaskForge brings system health information and practical controls into one desktop utility. The v2.2 beta explains live CPU, memory, commit pressure, and top processes; it can also request native Windows battery and energy reports without changing system settings.

Choose deliberately: v2.1.0 is the latest stable release. v2.2.0-beta.1 is a prerelease for early evaluation and may still contain unfinished behavior.
