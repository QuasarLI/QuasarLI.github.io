About Quasar Labs

Independent software, grounded in real needs.

Quasar Labs is the home of L’IRC’in, TaskForge, and the early-stage Quasar project.

Why we build

Good software can be ambitious without becoming noisy. We aim for tools with a clear purpose, understandable choices, and honest expectations. Projects are described by what exists today—not by inflated roadmaps.

Our approach

We prefer durable web foundations, accessible interfaces, and direct relationships with users. Some work is public on GitHub; paid editions and supporter purchases help sustain continued development.

What is current

L’IRC’in is live, TaskForge has public and supporter routes, and Quasar remains a research preview. The QuasarLI GitHub organization is the canonical home for public Quasar Labs repositories.
