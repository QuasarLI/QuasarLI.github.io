Know what you downloaded

Two signatures, different jobs.

01 · Archive provenance

Sigstore bundles

Every final ZIP and the checksum manifest has a keyless .sigstore.json bundle created by the repository’s GitHub Actions identity. This verifies release provenance and exact archive bytes.

02 · Android installation

APK signing

The APK inside each Android ZIP is signed with the Android release key so Android can verify its publisher and allow same-key upgrades. This is separate from Sigstore.

03 · Windows reputation

No Authenticode claim

Sigstore does not make a Windows executable Authenticode-signed and does not promise Microsoft SmartScreen reputation. Review the release notes and verify the ZIP before use.

Exact Sigstore identity to verify

Verify each ZIP or the checksum manifest against issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com and certificate identity https://github.com/QuasarLI/QuasarLI.github.io/.github/workflows/release-quasar.yml@refs/heads/main. A bundle proves that workflow identity signed those exact bytes; it does not establish Authenticode or APK signing.

Android 16 KB static verification: all four v0.4.0 APKs contain the four declared ABIs and pass 16 KB APK ZIP-alignment, ELF LOAD-alignment, and applicable GNU_RELRO boundary checks. This establishes the packaged files’ static layout—not successful execution on a 16 KB device. No Android emulator or physical-device runtime test is claimed; see the planned emulator procedure and its limits.

Deletion and evidence

Your copies require your decisions.

Deleting local data

Removing a backup from Quasar or deleting the app’s local data affects the selected local copy or app state. It does not automatically delete copies you exported, synced, shared, or stored with another provider. Use each destination provider’s deletion controls and account-retention process for those copies.

Deletion is not guaranteed secure erasure

Normal deletion may leave data in recycle bins, filesystem remnants, device snapshots, provider retention systems, or other backups. Quasar makes no promise of forensic secure erase. Protect sensitive archives with appropriate encryption and verify the result of any deletion process you rely on.

Preserve records when required

Do not delete or alter records subject to a legal hold, court order, regulatory request, investigation, audit, or reasonably anticipated proceeding. Deleting relevant records can constitute destruction of evidence or violate other obligations. If you are unsure, preserve the data and seek qualified legal advice before acting.
